abstract: |
  We present Paqocha, an innovative Spanish-to-Quechua (Cusco-Collao variety) machine translation system built on a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) framework. By integrating dynamic retrieval of aligned bilingual parallel corpus snippets and morphological templates, Paqocha mitigates vocabulary sparsity and hallucination issues commonly encountered when translating into low-resource, polysynthetic, and agglutinative indigenous languages.
