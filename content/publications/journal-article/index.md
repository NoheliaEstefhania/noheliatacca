abstract: |
  This study develops an interdisciplinary pedagogical framework repositioning traditional Andean dance not merely as folkloric performance, but as a measurable cognitive technology for Quechua Second Language Acquisition (SLA). Grounded in Embodied Cognition and Total Physical Response (TPR), the workshop "Kuntur Tusuyta Tususunchis" establishes a quantitative and qualitative assessment roadmap linking physical movement with phonological retention and morphosyntactic fluency.
